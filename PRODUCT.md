# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People who want a quick, friendly way to check which attack types work well or poorly against a Pokémon before or during play.

## Product Purpose

Let a user fuzzy-search for a Pokémon by name, choose the intended match, and immediately understand its defensive type matchups.

## Positioning

The app turns PokéAPI type data into a compact matchup readout that accounts for dual-type multipliers instead of making the user compare type charts manually.

## Operating Context

The primary flow is a short lookup on desktop or mobile: type a full or approximate name, select a suggestion, then scan weaknesses, resistances, and immunities.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Use PokéAPI as the live data source.
- Fuzzy-match Pokémon names and support keyboard selection.
- Calculate combined defensive multipliers for dual-type Pokémon.
- Present loading, empty, unavailable, and not-found states clearly.
- Keep the experience to one simple app surface.

## Brand Commitments

The interface should feel fun and recognizably inspired by the Pokémon franchise while remaining an unofficial utility and avoiding the official logo.

## Evidence on Hand

PokéAPI provides Pokémon names, sprites, types, and type damage relations. No testimonials, commercial claims, or proprietary assets were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Make the answer readable in seconds.
- Prefer clear multipliers over unexplained type-chart jargon.
- Keep fuzzy search forgiving and keyboard-friendly.
- Let live Pokémon artwork and type colors carry the theme.
